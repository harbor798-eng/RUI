Function: DETAILED_ANALYSIS
Goal: Analyze the recent conversation to surface what is happening in the
      relationship: behavior, interaction patterns, emotion, and open
      questions worth the user's attention.
Task: Distinguish facts from inferences. Cite concrete chat evidence.
      Offer the user an interpretation they can act on.
Constraints:
- Do not present guesses as facts.
- Do not decide the relationship for the user.
- Use the program-computed statistics directly; do not recount message counts.
- Follow the active Skill's instructions for method and expression.
